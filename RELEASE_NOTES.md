# VGRPresenter 0.0.4

## What's new

- A clearer view of your computer's health and performance.
- More color choices for the app.

## Improvements

- Settings, menus, and pop-up windows are easier to use.
- Startup is smoother, with updates shown while the app loads.
- Live output and NDI work more reliably.

## Fixes

- Opening Audio & Video settings no longer turns on audio monitoring.
- Adding an audio source is smoother.
- Pop-up windows no longer let clicks reach the screen behind them.
- Fixed several issues with saved settings, displays, and slides.

## Windows downloads

- **VGRPresenter-Setup-0.0.4.exe** — recommended installer.
- **VGRPresenter-win64.zip** — portable version; unzip it and open the app.

Requires Windows 10 or newer.
